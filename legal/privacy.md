**We don't collect personal data through this website.** No analytics, no cookies, no trackers, no accounts. And the apps we build are privacy-first by design: your data stays on your device and in your own iCloud — never on our servers.

**This policy covers `khassinx.com` only** — the umbrella site for the studio. Each KHASSINX app publishes its own privacy policy on its subdomain (e.g., `asvab.khassinx.com`). For how a specific app handles your data, see that app's policy.

## What this site does not do

- **No analytics.** No Google Analytics, no Plausible, no Fathom — not even first-party analytics.
- **No cookies.** The site sets no cookies of its own, and a normal visit sets none. Cloudflare's security layer can set a strictly necessary security cookie when its protection steps in (see our [Cookies](/legal/cookies/) page).
- **No third-party scripts.** No social embeds, no chat widgets, no ad pixels.
- **No accounts.** There is nothing to sign up for here.
- **No data sale or sharing.** There is no data to sell or share.

## What our hosts collect

**GitHub Pages** (our static file host) and **Cloudflare** (our network layer) automatically log standard HTTP request metadata — IP address, user-agent, timestamp, and requested URL — to serve and protect the site. We don't get user-level analytics from either. Their logs are governed by [GitHub's privacy policy](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) and [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## How the studio handles data across our apps

Everything we make is built the same way: **your data lives on your device and in your own iCloud account — not on servers we control.** We don't embed third-party tracking SDKs, and we don't sell or share your information. Apps that need a backend service operate without holding your personal data (for example, by using short-lived provider tokens rather than storing your details). For exactly what a specific app stores, read that app's privacy policy on its subdomain.

## Data controller

`khassinx.com` is operated by **KHASSINX LLC**, a Florida limited liability company (United States) — the data controller for this site. You can reach us at [`legal@khassinx.com`](mailto:legal@khassinx.com).

## Email contact

If you write to any `@khassinx.com` address, we receive your message and reply address through our email provider, and we keep that correspondence to handle the conversation and any follow-up. We don't add you to any mailing list. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

## Your rights

Browsing this site gives us no personal data, so a visit leaves nothing of yours to access or delete. If you emailed us, you can ask to see or delete that correspondence (see [Email contact](#email-contact)). For the rights you have under your local law — GDPR (EU/EEA), the UK, Spain's LOPDGDD, California's CCPA, other US states, and elsewhere — and how to exercise them, see **[Your Privacy Rights](/legal/your-rights/)**.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site or our infrastructure changes. Material changes are reflected in the effective date shown above.
