Apps built privately.
On purpose.

KhassinX is an independent studio crafting on-device AI experiences for Apple platforms. No tracking. No accounts. Pricing that respects you.

How we build

Six architectural principles enforced in code review, not slogans.

On-device intelligence

Apple Foundation Models running locally. Your data never leaves your device for AI inference.

Zero tracking

No analytics SDKs, no telemetry, no third-party trackers linked.

No account required

Apps work the moment you install them. No sign-up, no email collection.

Honest pricing

We charge fairly for what we make. Some apps are one-time purchases, some are subscriptions when they include ongoing services. Every app's pricing is explained up front — no dark patterns, no surprise renewals.

Native Apple Watch

Companion experiences where useful, not just notifications.

Liquid Glass

Built on Apple's latest design system across iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch — not a legacy theme.
