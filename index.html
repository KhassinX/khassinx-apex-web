Apps that keep your data on your device and in your iCloud.

KHASSINX is an independent studio making apps for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch. Your data stays on your device and in your own iCloud. There is no account to create, and nothing tracks you.

Every app we publish shows “Data Not Collected” on its App Store privacy label.

How we build

Six rules every app we publish follows.

No account.
Apps work the moment you install them. We never ask for your email.
Check: there is no sign‑in screen.
No tracking.
No analytics SDKs, no telemetry, no third‑party trackers.
Check: the App Store privacy label says “Data Not Collected”.
Your data stays yours.
It lives on your device and in your own iCloud account, not on servers we control.
Check: Infrastructure Providers names every company involved.
AI on your device.
When an app uses AI, it runs Apple’s on‑device model. Your data is not sent to an AI provider.
The price, up front.
You see what an app costs before you pay. No subscriptions, so nothing renews.
Native on every device.
Each app is built for every device it supports, with Apple’s current design, Liquid Glass. ASVAB Coach runs on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch.

What does reach us: crash reports and usage information, if you chose in your device settings to share them with app developers (Apple passes them on), and the emails you send us.
